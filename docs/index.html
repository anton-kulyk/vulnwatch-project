03 · SIGNAL PIPELINE

How observations become useful

VulnWatch is not a raw-output relay. Each stage adds a different kind of context, while preserving the distinction between an observable signal and a confirmed security conclusion.

STAGEQUESTIONTECHNICAL DETAILRESULT
ScopeWhat may be assessed?Target, intent, boundaries, exclusions, preview checkpoint.Authorized job
ObserveWhat is externally visible?HTTP/TLS behavior, headers, cookies, public surface and technology signals.Raw observations
NormalizeWhat belongs together?Common finding shape, source preservation, classification and deduplication.Comparable records
InterpretWhy might it matter?Correlation, evidence context, confidence, technical meaning and limitations.Finding candidates
PrioritizeWhat deserves attention first?Severity, impact, exposure context, asset importance and practical next action.Triage order
VerifyDid the signal change?Remediation feedback and a new authorized assessment for comparison.Follow-up state

04 · OUTPUTS

Built for decisions,
not alert volume.

Reports are intended to make technical findings easier to review and act on. A useful result connects an observation to evidence, context, impact, and a practical next step.

01

Finding context

What was observed, where it was observed, and which signal layer produced it.

02

Risk rationale

Why the observation may matter, with confidence and limitations kept visible.

03

Action path

What to investigate, remediate, or verify next — without pretending to provide a universal fix.
